# 🚀 QUICK START GUIDE - ETHIZA APPAREL

## 5-Minute Setup & First Use

---

## Step 1: Open the Website

**Option A - Start Page:**
Open: `pages/home.html` in your browser

**Option B - Direct Links:**
- Home: `pages/home.html`
- Shop: `pages/shop.html`
- Login: `pages/login.html`

---

## Step 2: Create Your Account (2 min)

1. Click **Login** button (👤) in top-right
2. Click **Create Account**
3. Fill the form:
   - **Full Name:** Your name
   - **Email:** Your email (must be unique)
   - **Password:** At least 6 characters
   - **Confirm Password:** Same as above
4. Click **Create Account**
5. ✅ Account created! Now login with these credentials

---

## Step 3: Login to Your Account (1 min)

1. Click **Login** button again
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. ✅ Welcome! You're logged in

---

## Step 4: Browse Products (1 min)

1. Click **Shop** in the menu
2. **See all products** in the grid
3. **Filters available:**
   - Filter by category (Dress, Tops, Ethnic, Casual, Party)
   - Filter by price range (slide the price slider)
4. Click **"Clear Filters"** to see all products

---

## Step 5: View Product Details (30 sec)

1. On Shop page, **click any product image** or **product name**
2. ✨ Product detail modal pops up showing:
   - Product image
   - Full description
   - Price and rating
   - Stock status
3. Click **X** or outside to close

---

## Step 6: Add to Wishlist (30 sec)

1. On Shop page, click the **heart (❤️)** button on any product
2. Heart changes color (when item is in wishlist)
3. ❤️ button appears in header showing count
4. Click again to remove

**View Wishlist:**
- Click ❤️ icon in header to see all saved items

---

## Step 7: Add to Cart (30 sec)

1. On Shop page, click **"Add to Cart"** button on any product
2. 🛒 Cart button shows count in header
3. Can add same item multiple times (quantity increases)

**View Cart:**
- Click 🛒 icon in header
- Or go to Cart page from menu

---

## Step 8: Shopping Cart Actions (1 min)

1. Go to **Cart** page
2. See all items with:
   - Product name, price, category
   - Quantity controls (+/-)
   - Item subtotal
   - Remove button
3. **Order Summary** shows total amount
4. Actions:
   - **Increase quantity:** Click + button
   - **Decrease quantity:** Click - button
   - **Remove item:** Click "Remove" button
   - **Total auto-updates** with each change

---

## Step 9: Checkout (2 min)

1. In Cart page, click **"Proceed to Checkout"**
2. Fill **Shipping Address:**
   - Name
   - Email
   - Phone (10 digits)
   - Address
   - City
   - State
   - Pincode (6 digits)
3. Select **Payment Method:**
   - Cash on Delivery (COD)
   - Credit/Debit Card
   - PayPal
   - Razorpay
4. Click **"Place Order"**
5. ✅ Order confirmation page shows:
   - Order ID
   - Total amount
   - Success message

---

## Step 10: View Your Profile (1 min)

1. Click your **Profile button (👤)** in header
2. See your account details:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Member since date
3. See **Order History:**
   - All orders you've placed
   - Order ID, amount, status
   - Order date

---

## Step 11: Logout (10 sec)

1. Click **Logout button (🚪)** in header
2. You're logged out
3. Login button appears again

## 🆘 Common Issues & Fixes

### "Product modal won't open?"
- ✅ Make sure you click on **product image** or **product name**
- ✅ Click on the product CARD area, not blank space
- ✅ Refresh page if it's still not working

### "Cart not updating?"
- ✅ Click **Add to Cart** button
- ✅ Check header for 🛒 badge with count
- ✅ Go to Cart page to view items
- ✅ Refresh page to reload

### "Wishlist not showing?"
- ✅ Click ❤️ **heart button** to add items
- ✅ Click ❤️ **icon in header** to view wishlist
- ✅ Heart should change appearance when item added

### "Can't login?"
- ✅ Make sure email is **exactly** as registered
- ✅ Check **password spelling** (case-sensitive)
- ✅ Minimum password is **6 characters**
- ✅ Refresh page and try again
